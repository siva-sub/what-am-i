Sheet 6 of 12 · player aid · cut into 4

What Am I?

Your card is face-out. You may not read it. Nobody may tell you. Nobody may confirm.

Your turn — pick one

Ask
Whisper to one player: “what am I?” The answer is private.
Refusing is legal — and a tell.
Declare
Say your role out loud. The table reads your card.
Swap
Spend a token, draw from the pile. New card goes face-out.

Token — two uses

  • ↺  Swap your card
  • ↺  Clear your own MARK

Declaring

  • Correct → score its value · round ends
  • Wrong → you are OUT. Name your source; that player becomes MARKED.
  • MARKED → you cannot declare

Other

  • No declaring in the first circuit
  • Glimpse your own card → declare at once
  • Eliminated → WITNESS: once per round, publicly announce any one card, true or lie
  • Full circuit with no declaration → everyone declares at once
  • First to 8 points wins the match

ASSEMBLY — score every line with a dead pen before folding. Fold all creases to the same side first, then glue the two tabs inward. Panel order is TOP / FRONT / BACK / BASE, with the extra right-hand panel being the tuck lid that folds over the top. Interior is 62 × 92 × 22 mm — comfortably loose for 12 sleeved cards.

IF THE LID IS TOO FIDDLY, cut the whole net apart and use the four large panels as a single wrap-around band held shut with a paper clip. Same protection, no folding.

VARIANT RULES
HOW TO PLAY ✱ ABILITIES
Swap exactly ONE base card for its ✱ counterpart before dealing. Players can see the ✱ on the holder’s forehead. The holder may only invoke the ability if they deduce or gamble that they hold it!
